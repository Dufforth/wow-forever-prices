Help

Profit is per craft, using the best way to sell after the 5% auction cut. Prices use the market average where Blizzard's data has one, and the lowest listing elsewhere. Reagents use the cheapest of auction, vendor or crafting them yourself. Disenchant values are expected averages from Classic tables, so check them in game.

Help

Items worth more as enchanting materials than they cost, after the 5% auction cut on the materials. Values are expected averages from the Classic disenchant tables, by item level, so single results vary and a small profit can turn into a small loss. Materials with no auction price are left out, so high-level gear can be missing. Bind on pickup gear and items with random bonuses are left out. Select an item for its full material odds.

    How it works
    1. Install: download Dufforth AH Scanner and unzip it into World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns, then restart the game. If you have an older TentTimer folder there, delete it.
    2. Choose items: add the items you want here, press Copy list for addon, then type /ahscan list in game, paste and save. Do this again whenever your list changes.
    3. Scan: opening the auction house runs a light scan of materials, consumables, rare and epic gear, popular crafted gear and your items, in at most 30 seconds. Type /ahscan for the full scan. Press Scan my list for just your items. To turn the automatic scan off, type /ahscan auto off.
    4. See prices: type /reload, press Load scan here and pick WTF\Account\(your account)\SavedVariables\DufforthAHScanner.lua.

    Your list and scans stay in this browser. They aren't shared with other people.

    Asking prices, not completed sales. Market is the average of the cheapest 15%.
    About this data

    Prices come from Blizzard's API, about once an hour. Markets it can't provide use player scans from the public auctionhouse.fyi API, where Market just repeats the lowest price. Recipes come from the game client's tables through wago.tools. Item details come from Wowhead. To refresh prices yourself, open the auction house in game, wait for the automatic scan, type /reload, then press Load scan here and pick World of Warcraft\_forever_\WTF\Account\(your account)\SavedVariables\DufforthAHScanner.lua. Scans and favorites are saved in this browser only. Not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment.

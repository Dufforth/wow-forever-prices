How profit is worked out

Profit is per craft, using the best way to sell after the 5% auction cut. Prices are the single lowest listing, so one unusual listing can mislead. Reagents use the cheapest of auction, vendor or crafting them yourself. Disenchant values are expected averages from Classic tables, so check them in game.

How deals are worked out

Gear for sale below the expected value of its disenchanted materials, after the 5% auction cut. Results vary from item to item, so a small profit can turn into a small loss. Bind on pickup gear and items with random bonuses are left out.

Prices come from players scanning the auction house, through the public auctionhouse.fyi API. They are the lowest asking price, not completed sales, and only as fresh as the latest scan. Recipes come from the game client's tables through wago.tools. Favorites are saved in this browser only. Not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment.

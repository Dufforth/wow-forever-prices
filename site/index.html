About this data

Prices come from the public auctionhouse.fyi API and are only as fresh as the latest scan. Recipes come from the game client's tables through wago.tools. Item details come from Wowhead. To refresh prices yourself, open the auction house in game, press Scan prices, type /reload, then press Load scan here and pick World of Warcraft\_forever_\WTF\Account\(your account)\SavedVariables\TentTimer.lua. Scans and favorites are saved in this browser only. Not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment.

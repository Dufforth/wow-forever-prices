Help

Items worth more as enchanting materials than they cost, after the 5% auction cut on the materials. Values are expected averages from the Classic disenchant tables, by item level, so single results vary and a small profit can turn into a small loss. Materials with no auction price are left out, so high-level gear can be missing. Bind on pickup gear and items with random bonuses are left out. Select an item for its full material odds.

About this data

Prices come from Blizzard's API, about once an hour. Markets it can't provide come from the public auctionhouse.fyi API, where Market just repeats the lowest price. Recipes come from the game client's tables through wago.tools. Item details come from Wowhead. Favorites, columns and sorting are saved in this browser only. Not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment.

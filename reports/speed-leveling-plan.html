1. Which sample guide saves more time

RestedXP's free guides stop at level 22, so all three routes are compared on the same stretch: an Orc Warrior from level 1 in Durotar to level 22 (Durotar, the Barrens, Stonetalon, Ashenvale). Each route goes through the same time model:

  • running at 7 yards a second between steps;
  • flights at 25 yards a second, plus 20 seconds;
  • 10 seconds per NPC talk and 20 seconds per objective kill;
  • a hearthstone is 15 seconds;
  • grinding at the end for any experience the quests don't cover.

The guides add walking waypoints between stops and our route doesn't, so the fair comparison counts one map point per step for all three.

RestedXP saves the most time: about 1.9 hours less than Zygor (13%) and 1.3 hours less than our route. It's quicker because it runs a third less. It flies 26 times and uses the hearthstone 12 times, so long walks back to town become a cast or a flight. Zygor does the most quests but walks everywhere.

Our route is shorter than Zygor's but does too few quests: it falls about 14,000 experience short of 22 and has to grind. The fix isn't fewer quests; it's RestedXP's travel tricks on top of a fuller quest list.

Totals only; no step or text of either guide is copied. Quest experience is Classic's (paid out at full value), and the time model is a simplification applied equally to all three routes.

  • New zones. Mount Hyjal (Summit of Eternity, Tainted Foothills), Riverglades (Rog'mar, Powderfuse Port, Farholde Keep) and Zephras Isle have flight points in Forever's data. No Classic guide knows them, and that includes Zygor and RestedXP.
  • Forever's own quests have no public details. The client lists their numbers only: no names, levels, NPCs or places. No guide can plan them from public data today. Our addon already shows them from your quest log, and it could record them as you play (who gives them, where, what they ask, where they're handed in). That would make ours the only guide covering Forever's own content.
  • Forever's experience boosts. Well Fed foods add 5% kill experience on top of their stat. An "Elixir of Endless Experience" (+200%) and a "Burst of Experience" exist in the data; the elixir isn't sold on the auction house.

3. Where our route loses time

On 1-22, 84 quests are in all three routes. Another 39 quests are in both sample guides but not in ours. Together they're worth 43,100 quest experience, about a fifth of what it takes to reach 22. That's why our route has to grind.

The best of them include Deviate Eradication, Cry of the Thunderhawk, Enraged Thunder Lizards, Deviate Hides, Hidden Enemies and Returning the Lost Satchel. Both guides also take the Wailing Caverns quests around levels 17-22, a large experience boost for anyone running the dungeon.

The second loss is travel. Our planner never plans a flight or a hearthstone. RestedXP's 26 flights and 12 hearthstones are why it runs 50 km less than we do on the same levels.

4. The plan

The goal is to beat RestedXP's 12.4 hours on 1-22 with the same time model, then carry the same rules through 60, with Zygor as the 22-60 yardstick. Every route build prints the scorecard, so each change is measured instead of guessed.

  1. Keep the experience both guides keep. Value quests by quest experience plus objective-kill experience against time, and treat a quest both reference guides use as a strong hint to look at why we drop it. Target: at least the quest experience RestedXP gets, so 22 comes without grinding.
  2. Hearthstone batching. The planner sets the hearthstone at the hub it returns to most, and plans "use your Hearthstone" whenever the walk back is longer than the cast. The addon already says when it's ready.
  3. Flights in the plan. Model the 328 flight routes from Forever's data. Fly whenever the flight plus the walk to the flight master beats walking.
  4. Place summoned monsters. Read the summoning scripts in the Classic database to place the 7 missing quests and others like them later in the game.
  5. Class quests and training in the route. Train when you pass your trainer at a new level, and take class quests when they're on the way.
  6. Optional dungeon paths. A "with dungeons" route that takes Wailing Caverns, Shadowfang Keep and the others with their quests, for players who group. The solo route stays the default.
  7. Forever's own content. The addon records Forever's own quests as you play them: where you got each one, its objectives, and where you handed it in. Recorded quests join the route. Add Mount Hyjal and Riverglades as soon as their quests are known. Neither sample guide can do this.

Steps 1 to 3 are where most of the time is. With all 39 missing quests and RestedXP's travel tricks, the model puts our 1-22 at about 11.5 to 12 hours, an estimate to confirm with the scorecard after each change.

What would improve ours most:

  1. Forever's own quests. The addon's recorder (4.32) learns them as you play, and the route picks them up after each import. A faster path: RestedXP's guides and database are under a Creative Commons non-commercial, share-alike licence. We could use their Forever quest places with credit, but only if our addon is shared under the same licence. That's your call; Zygor's data stays off limits.
  2. Learn flight paths only when they're on the way, and set the hearthstone at every hub the route returns to. That closes most of the travel gap with RestedXP.
  3. Say so when there's no route for your start (Zephras Isle, Forever dungeons) instead of falling back to Durotar.
